Singapore's Living Waters

An interactive guide to the marine life living in Singapore's waters.

This project aims to connect Singaporeans with the rich marine biodiversity in our waters by anchoring each organism to a physical location. This is done to show that marine life in Singapore is tangible, diverse and accessible, in hopes of encouraging locals to visit these spots to explore for themselves.

How to use

  • Pins are real dive or shore sites
  • Choose a type of habitat by choosing from the legend or click directly on a pin
  • Select a site to meet its inhabitants
  • Click into an inhabitant to see its species card and learn more

Where the data comes from

  • Trophic levels for fish: FishBase
  • Invertebrates: SeaLifeBase and the feeding-ecology literature
  • Map data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, served by OpenFreeMap, drawn with MapLibre GL JS
  • Sites and species selected from Singapore shore and reef survey material

Images and footage

Species media is still being gathered. Where a photograph or clip appears, its source and licence are credited on the frame.

** Depth ranges are working estimates. Only two species in this guide had a depth recorded in their source material; the rest were assigned from their habitat while the guide is being built, and should not be read as measurements.
